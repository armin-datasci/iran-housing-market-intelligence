## D01 Evidence Note - Monetary Scale vs. Currency Label

D01 deliberately separates **numerical-scale validity** from **absolute denomination naming**. The first is empirically testable inside the project and is strongly supported by raw/typed parity, factor-of-ten anomaly tests, rent-deposit transformation structure, and cross-market sale/rental coherence. The second is not identifiable under a global rescaling without authoritative external source documentation. Consequently, `scale=1` is a data-supported analytical decision, whereas `Toman` remains an operational label with the explicit status `toman_assumed_unconfirmed`.
